How this report was made

Traced to data. The checker confirmed that each number, date and quote in the findings appears in the facts it cites. This does not prove each label is right, and it cannot catch a fact that is misread.

  • Labels by source: llm: 972.
  • Rules agreed with the LLM on 58% of the sentiment labels the LLM produced.
  • 0 comments are flagged for human review in labelled_comments.csv.
  • LLM use: 48 calls plus 0 from cache, 69,564 tokens in, 48,031 tokens out, 847 seconds.
  • Phone numbers, long ID numbers and emails were masked before any model saw the text. Names and street addresses are not masked.

Decisions the agent made, in order

  1. ingestRead 9,093 rows. Columns detected, text: tweet_text.
  2. ingestUsing a random sample of 1,000 of the 9,093 rows, as asked.
  3. cleanStatus of rows: {'ok': 1000}. 972 distinct texts remain, so repeats are labelled once. Languages: {'English': 1000}.
  4. planLLM 'gemini-3.5-flash-lite' responded. 972 distinct comments at 25 per call need about 39 calls. The budget is 200 calls.
  5. discoverFound 5 topics in the data: Google parties, Apple store, iPhone usage, iPad products, Android apps.
  6. classifyThe budget allows it, so the LLM labels all 972 comments in this step.
  7. check8.8% of comments fit no topic. Accepting the topic list.
  8. analyseComputed 26 facts in code. The LLM labelled 100% of analysed comments. Alerts: none. Spikes: none.
  9. investigateLLM chose more_reasons on 'iPhone usage' (Need to understand the specific complaints regarding autocorrect and battery to assess severity.). Found: Reasons for negative comments on iPhone usage: "complains about iPhone autocorrect and battery" (6); "frustrated with Twitter app failure" (2); "app drains iPhone battery
  10. investigateLLM chose platform_split on 'iPhone usage' (Determine where the iPhone battery and autocorrect complaints are concentrated to target fixes effectively.). Found: Only one platform has comments on iPhone usage.
  11. investigateLLM decided there is enough evidence: We have sufficient detail on the negative reasons for iPhone usage and overall low negative volume..
  12. writeLLM wrote draft 1: 5 points, 4 actions.
  13. verifyChecker passed draft 1: every number, date and quote traces back to the facts it cites.
