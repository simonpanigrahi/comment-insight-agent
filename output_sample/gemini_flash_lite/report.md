## Key findings

- Battery life received 933 comments, which is 23.0% of all analysed comments, with 76.7% being negative and an impact score of 1,392.5. (F9)
- Negative comments for Battery life rose from about 16.4 per day to about 41.3 per day from 2026-09-23, representing a 2.5 times increase. (F18)
- Words far more common in negative Battery life comments from 2026-09-23 than before it include v1.2, update, half, software, backup, and cut, though this shows they may be linked rather than a proven cause. (F57)
- Safety reports account for 38 comments with a total engagement of 2,328, first seen on 2026-09-15. (F8)
- Legal or fraud reports comprise 16 comments with a total engagement of 98, first seen on 2026-09-19, where 5 were judged false alarms by the LLM. (F4)
- Privacy or security reports total 7 comments with a total engagement of 49, first seen on 2026-09-16. (F6)
