## Key findings

- Sentiment across 1,000 comments includes 55.1% negative (551), 15.9% positive (159), and 29.0% neutral (290), with complaints making up 53.9% of what people are doing in their comments. (F2, F3)
- Negative comments rose in 6 topics at about the same time starting around 2015-02-22, indicating a wider event may be behind this rather than separate problems in each topic. (F22)
- Customer service negative comments rose from about 9.2 per day to about 27.5 per day from 2015-02-23, and Flight status negative comments rose from about 4.3 per day to about 13.4 per day from 2015-02-20. (F16, F17)
- Baggage and boarding negative comments rose from about 5.8 per day to about 16.7 per day from 2015-02-22, and Flight cancellations negative comments rose from about 4.0 per day to about 10.7 per day from 2015-02-22. (F18, F19)
- Hold times negative comments rose from about 1.8 per day to about 9.8 per day from 2015-02-21, and Customer support negative comments rose from about 2.3 per day to about 8.0 per day from 2015-02-23. (F20, F21)
- Flagged by the LLM as urgent reports are 7 comments with total engagement 0 and first seen 2015-02-19. (F4)
